# Sealed retrieval holdout methodology

The holdout preparer worked before caption authors received any held-out query. The fixed evaluation population is the 57 memories with nonempty bodies in the audited f190–f213 readback. Each live body was read through the evidence GET endpoint and checked against its audited memory ID. The broader census was sampled for frame, problem, role, search-result and retained-shelf variety when choosing task domains.

Queries were written as natural mathematical or formalization goals that a student or scribe might issue. They were not copied from memory names or hooks. Judgments distinguish direct relevance, suggested-only applicability, and controls for which no memory in the fixed population should match. Every positive or marginal judgment names an ID in the fixed population and has a one-line rationale in the sealed file. Suggested-only queries have no directly relevant judgment. Controls have neither relevant nor marginal judgments.

The previously tuned “ODE uniqueness endpoint” development probe was excluded. The sealed EDN remains outside all repositories. This directory records only its digest and aggregate counts, so caption authors can work without seeing the evaluation queries while later evaluators can verify that the holdout has not changed.
