## The question

> Can a community of humans and machines doing open-ended work leave an
> account of itself structured enough that the work can steer itself, so
> that activity becomes inference rather than motion?

Bergson's line is the method: "What we are doing is discovering problems; a
problem stated clearly enough can actually be solved." The question above is
the problem stated. If the answer is to be yes, four things have to hold.
Each is a requirement; each is kept by a way of working; each has work open
under it today.

## Therefore 1: records carry warrant

An assertion severed from its derivation cannot be checked, learned from, or
recovered. So the first requirement is that every record the community
leaves carries the derivation it came from. The work cannot steer itself on
conclusions whose warrant was discarded.

**The way of working: nothing is accepted on say-so.** The Producer of a
change supplies its test as part of the Product: the warrant (the shas, the
environment fingerprint, a fresh log, a typed none for absent parts). The
Place is the test registry. The monitor is the reviewer, whose first job is
the record check and whose real job is test adequacy. The sanction is
graduated: a run without a warrant is the dirty tree, worth fixing, never
grounds to withhold acceptance. Producers may modify the tests (principle
4B): a reviewer who finds a green-but-vacuous check replaces it.

## Therefore 2: one queryable self-account

Knowing what exists and its state, with provenance, at stack scale. So the
second requirement is that there is one account of the stack that can be
asked, and that an absence in it is a declared hole rather than silence.

**The way of working: everything that exists is on the map, or it is a
hole.** The Place is the substrate the cascade-real endpoint reads. Its
condition is monitored (principle 4B, monitoring the resource) by five
standards the endpoint reports on every read: regenerates, evidence,
reconstitution, honest holes, composed. The monitors are accountable and
named: each dimension has an owner (O1 claude-2, O2 claude-1, O3 and O5
claude-4, O4 and O7 claude-10). The boundary rule (1B) is that a section
whose read fails is shown as failed, not dropped. A violation is any piece
of infrastructure that is in use and not on the map, or on the map with no
provenance.

## Therefore 3: feedback reaches every participant

The loop's human and stakeholder edges are where learning silently dies. So
the third requirement is that a result is not delivered until the people and
agents it affects have it, and the account records who has it.

**The way of working: a send is not a receipt.** The participants are named
per episode: author, reviewer, owner, affected consumer, dispatcher,
monitor. Transport acceptance is weaker than receipt, which is weaker than
acknowledgement; the instance names which threshold it uses. The conflict
arena (principle 6) is the whistle: when bells cross, one party calls and the
other answers. Rule changes (principle 3) are operator rulings recorded
verbatim with their design consequences, and agents propose changes through
a named channel and see them ruled on. The reply proforma marks are the
operator-facing half of this: a reply that cannot be read as an intent
cannot be wired to the turn it answers.

## Therefore 4: accountable next-action choice, including goal formation

Deciding what to do next, accountably, under uncertainty, with the goals
themselves formable rather than given. So the fourth requirement is that
when the machine chooses, it says why in terms of ways of choosing, and the
choice can be refused.

**The way of working: a selection gives a reason over policies, and a
click can be refused.** The participants are Giraffe (reads the field and
says what unblocks what), the War Machine (selects, with G computed over
ways of selecting), Joe (rules on standing), and the seats (act). The
congruence rule (2A) is that selection reads who can act: a task held by
Joe is not a candidate for a seat, however large its cone. Benefits are
proportional to effort (2B): a click earns credit for work and is refunded
for a repair check. The sanctions are graduated: a refusal is typed; an
injury is opened in the ledger; a medic certifies the repair; the loop
stops. Nesting (8): the outer selection is itself a cascade over the inner
cascade, and the Lean clause for a selection is written before the click.
