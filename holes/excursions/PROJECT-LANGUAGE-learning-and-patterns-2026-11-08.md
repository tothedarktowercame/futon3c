# Two project languages for the next floor: learning, and the Alexandrian line (draft, 2026-11-08)

DRAFT by claude-6 for Joe to edit (2026-11-08). Joe: "what we're doing with
FUTON is making the benefits of the Alexandrian way of working more concrete &
accessible … let's work on the learning line, but let's also work on the
META/Alexandrian/pattern-based line." Method from
`forward-model/the-forward-model-is-a-pattern-cascade`: "Characterize the
future build as a typed cascade of design patterns … holes are patterns that
should exist and do not, rather than as a list of missions."

Form, after Alexander: each language is a short sequence of patterns from
larger to smaller. Each entry gives the pattern (library id, or **HOLE** when
the library has none), one line of what it asks, and the open items that
apply it. An open item that applies no pattern here is set aside for this
floor, not deleted. Closed missions are the floor below: what is built.
Item lists come from the 2026-11-08 keyword pass and title search; they are
first placements, not readings.

Lineage, for orientation (not a claim of credit): A Pattern Language (1977);
Cunningham's WikiWikiWeb (1995), built for the Portland Pattern Repository;
the PLoP conferences; PlanetMath (2002–); Peeragogy; FUTON.

## What the two languages share

L2/P2 are the same pattern (peeragogy/pattern-language), and L10/P12 are the
same hole: no one outside the builder has used either yet. Joe 2026-11-08: "yes
because we haven't actually finished the first real working demo". Where the
two meet is the first demo: a learner using the tutor, and a group using the
pattern language, are both "people outside" in the same sense.
