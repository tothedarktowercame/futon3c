Reading of the two roles (claude-6, for Joe to correct):
- **Program.** Cascade Live read top to bottom is one business (Hyperreal
  Enterprises) written as a cascade.
- **Cone.** Read as a cone, its apex is a coordination layer with a map into
  each member business's cascade; where two members share a function the maps
  must agree. Arxana's typed links are the maps; the agreement on a shared
  function is the co-benefit ledger.
- **Interpreter.** The War Machine chooses the next action from a cascade
  under a given C. Run with a member's C and no actuation it simulates (a
  twin); with actuation it operates.

## What it shares with the other languages

- B15 is the same hole as L10, P12 and the N crown: nobody but the builder
  has used it yet. Here "someone else" is a second business.
- B9 is where the community language's *cui bono* hole and Joe's axiom

  co-benefit ledger records who benefited from whose work, and members paying
  for the shared function is the sustaining context.

  (2026-11-08) as the precondition for the paper-alignment work.
