# Codex Scribe pattern library — f184

These patterns cover the certified departure from a proposed direct Kummer or
normalizer computation: control automorphisms of a simple extension through a
small intermediate field and a degree obstruction.

## math-formalization/exclude-relative-automorphisms-by-forbidden-cyclotomic-subextension

- **Trigger:** A simple extension of prime relative degree is generated by an
  element whose prime power lies in the base, and an automorphism over the base
  might move the generator by a root of unity.
- **Move:** Divide the image of the generator by the generator.  If the quotient
  is nontrivial, its cyclotomic equation gives a proper subextension whose
  degree is incompatible with the prime relative degree; rule this out with
  minimal-polynomial bounds and the finrank tower formula.
- **Why it works:** The quotient converts the nonlinear ambiguity among roots
  into a root-of-unity element.  Its minimal polynomial forces a cyclotomic
  subfield, while multiplicativity of relative finranks forbids that subfield
  inside an extension of coprime degree.

## math-formalization/restrict-an-automorphism-by-proving-it-fixes-an-adjoin

- **Trigger:** An automorphism is bundled over a smaller base field, but a
  relative-extension lemma requires the same map bundled over an intermediate
  field generated by elements already known to be fixed.
- **Move:** Prove pointwise fixation of the intermediate field using
  `IntermediateField.adjoin_induction`, then reuse the automorphism structure
  with a new `commutes'` field to obtain an `AlgEquiv` over the intermediate
  field.
- **Why it works:** Fixing the adjoin generators and the original base forces
  fixation of every element constructed by the field operations, which is
  exactly the scalar-compatibility obligation of the rebundled algebra
  equivalence.
