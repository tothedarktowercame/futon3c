# Codex Scribe pattern library — f73

This pattern was added because reviewed mathematics-memory search returned no
coherent parent for classifying homomorphisms out of a finite cyclic group by
the image of its generator.

## math-formalization/classify-cyclic-actions-through-the-generator-image

- **Trigger:** A homomorphism from a finite cyclic group into an automorphism
  group must be classified, and the possible target automorphisms can be
  enumerated concretely.
- **Move:** Reduce the homomorphism to the image of a chosen generator.  Use
  the generator's order to constrain that image, eliminate the impossible
  enumerated automorphisms by evaluating their required power at a generator
  of the acted-on cyclic group, and prove equality of the surviving
  homomorphisms on the singleton generator with the dense-set extensionality
  API.
- **Why it works:** A homomorphism sends an element of finite order to an
  element whose order divides it, while a cyclic source is generated by one
  element.  Thus the order equation gives a finite classification of the
  generator image, and agreement there determines the whole homomorphism.
