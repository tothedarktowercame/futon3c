## First packet: six historical solves, at most three candidate patterns

Inspect six unsealed development solutions, chosen as three pairs sharing a
plausible intermediate mathematical construction. Prefer representation from
both Codex and Zai where execution records establish authorship; unknown author
is an explicit category, never inferred from a filename or apparent style.
Use at least two mathematical areas. Avoid making a93A01 the main case: the
ongoing pilot already covers it. Start with the existing censuses and retained
sources rather than scanning all campaigns indiscriminately.

For each solve, record exact problem/statement variant, solution/source revision,
verification evidence and its limits, author provenance, and a compact structured
proof reconstruction. Identify the method that makes each important intermediate
construction possible; distinguish ordinary logical steps, existing patterns,
reviewed memory/example attachments and proposed missing descriptions. Preserve
conditional or repaired statements; no-placeholder source is not automatically
a faithful solution to the original question.

Read the existing library descriptions and bounded related memory records before
claiming a gap. List search terms and candidate entries actually examined. A
bounded search miss is not exhaustive library absence. Classify each finding as:
existing method reusable as written; discoverability/example-link gap; existing
method needs clearer conditions or granularity; or genuinely candidate new
method within the inspected scope. Do not count renaming as a new pattern.

Propose at most three unpublished patterns. Each needs TWO distinct problem
witnesses (otherwise retain as single-example hypothesis), definitions and exact
applicability conditions, the construction supplied, a countercondition or
failure example, existing-pattern overlap, and source/proof-node citations.
Where available, historical failed attempts may explain why the intermediate
method would help, but do not invent failures or claim counterfactual improvement
as observed. The question is how the solutions can be taught/reconstructed as
pattern cascades, not merely which theorem names occur in them.
