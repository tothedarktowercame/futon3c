## Which finite-net step works / fails (source-lesson.md mapping)

Source lesson S chain, applied to (X, f):
1. **"Choose the tolerance δ for ε=1."** — WORKS. δ := 1 (or any δ≤1): d(m,n)<1 ⇒ m=n ⇒ |f(m)−f(n)|=0<1, for all m,n. Uniform continuity of f supplies this without issue.
2. **"Centers c_i, i<m, every point within δ of one center" — i.e. a finite δ-net.** — FAILS. This step uses the *general condition* "total boundedness means a finite radius-r net for every r>0, with centers in the space." X is bounded but **not** totally bounded: the δ=1 balls are singletons {n} (for δ≤1), so any δ-net must contain every natural number — no finite one exists. This is the exact break point; nothing before it fails and nothing after it can start.
3. **"max_i |f(c_i)| finite via finiteness of the net."** — BLOCKED by step 2 (vacuously: no finite net, no finite maximum). No other step of the lesson fails.
