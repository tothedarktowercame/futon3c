## Authorized deliverable and execution

Prepare and execute a small development pilot, not a live-loop installation.
Use three clearly labelled cases: good existing-pattern coverage, a tempting
method with a missing prerequisite, and a candidate library gap. Select ordinary
unsealed development mathematics, including a cross-problem target for a method
taught on another example. a93A01 can be the teaching/warm-up source; it is familiar
and not evidence of fresh transfer. Do not use sealed holdout material.

First bounded milestone: freeze the three-case task/teaching manifest and carry
one genuine source-to-target Student walkthrough through an initial attempt,
observed difficulty, TA feedback if needed, and revised proof or documented
failure. Continue remaining cases within the same pilot commission, preserving
case-specific exposure records. Same-Student learning across cases is intended;
it is not a fresh independent session per case or a performance comparison.

TA output precedes Student attempts: definitions, top-down obligation cascade,
pattern IDs/revisions, required conditions, explicit mathematical constructions,
staged hints and withheld reference solution. Inspect existing canonical patterns
and reviewed example links; distinguish a bounded search miss from proven absence.
Do not promote a new pattern on one example or claim memory use without a read.

Student first receives the task and bounded pattern discovery access, not the
reference solution or full TA cascade. Use an explicit allowlist of provided
materials and record tool access; shared-filesystem instructions are not a claim
of technical isolation. If access restrictions cannot be enforced, disclose that
limit and any actual exposure. No answer-containing handoff should be sent to the
Student before its initial attempt. Hints are delivered one stage at a time.

Record search results, actual reads, premise checks, attempted construction,
proof/checker outcomes, hint exposures and revisions. Keep retrieved/read/claimed
used/proof-supported used distinct. Classify feedback as retrieval miss,
applicability gap, condition gap, construction gap, library error, verification
failure or undetermined, based on evidence. A correct rejection is useful; a
citation is not a proof. Report whether a construction transferred, what required
repair, and what remains unknown. No causal efficacy or prevalence claim from
this pilot; no fabricated Student transcript.
