\begin{abstract}\noindent
This is the fourth note in the capability-proof series (APM, arXiv,
War Machine), and it is reflexive: its subject is the instrument the
other three lean on. The diagram prover exists in \emph{versions},
and the honest statement of what the programme can certify is
indexed by version: the Pearl-inspired engine (DAG-level:\ backdoor,
front-door, general identification, transport via selection
diagrams, deterministic counterfactuals) is implemented,
oracle-triangulated, and \emph{sufficient for every causal claim the
APM proof makes}; the War Machine proof's load-bearing claims ---
one-way signalling at the consent gate, no-signalling exogeneity,
silence as an open continuation --- are not expressible at that
tier and demand a Kissinger-inspired version
($\Caus[-]$/BV:\ higher-order causal categories, where the stack's
own dark-tower excursion has already mapped the connectives); and a
Fong-inspired tier (synthetic, Markov-categorical:\ intervention as
string-diagram surgery, do-calculus without reference to a
particular graph semantics) is registered for capabilities beyond
both. The organizing discipline is a new refusal species,
\emph{not-expressible-at-tier}: the prover refuses claims its
current logic cannot state, those refusals are typed and logged, and
the ladder grows exactly where the client proofs are hungry ---
demand-paged logic acquisition, with the refusal log as the demand
signal and its gain instrumented per WR-27.
\end{abstract}

\section{The claim, read constructively}

The headline claim --- \emph{the programme's capability claims are
certifiable} --- read constructively, asserts a procedure: handed
any claim appearing in a client capability proof, the procedure
either routes it to a prover version whose logic expresses it and
whose machinery identifies or measures it (returning a receipt), or
returns a \emph{typed refusal} stating exactly why not ---
not-identified (the logic expresses it, the data cannot), or
not-expressible-at-tier (no formula of the current logic states the
claim). The proof object is the version ladder plus the router;
the refusal log is its odometer.

Reflexivity requires one guardrail, stated at the outset: this
document's claims about the prover are \emph{not} certified by the
prover. They are certified by the client proofs' own certificates
--- the external anchor --- and by ordinary mechanical means
(oracle agreement, executed checks). A prover licensing its own
capability claims would be laundering; the series' whole discipline
exists to prevent that shape.

\section{The ladder}

\subsection*{D1 --- Pearl-inspired (implemented)}

DAG-level causal inference: backdoor and front-door adjustment,
general identification, transportability via selection diagrams,
deterministic counterfactuals. \textbf{Warrant:}
\warrant{mechanical}/\warrant{replicated} --- triangulated against
independent DAG-level oracles; the six standing receipts
self-answered (2026-08-03); refusal discipline exercised (the
engine declines non-identified contrasts rather than estimating
them). \textbf{What it certifies in the client proofs:} the APM
note's causal layer is D1-complete --- the $do(\textrm{frame})$
controlled contrast (N4), the APM$\to$BPM transport statement (N6),
and the mediation-style questions in the memory lane are all
expressible and their identification receipts issued or issuable at
this tier. The arXiv note's hazard-incidence claims likewise. D1 is
not the bottleneck anywhere in the first two client proofs; that is
a finding, not a boast --- it locates the demand frontier precisely
at the third.

\subsection*{D2 --- Kissinger-inspired (registered)}

Higher-order causal categories --- $\Caus[\mathcal{C}]$ --- which
are models of BV-logic (Simmons \& Kissinger, arXiv:2205.11219):
$\otimes$ types non-signalling composition, $\parr$ types
unconstrained interaction, and the self-dual non-commutative
$\seq$ types \emph{one-way} signalling. The stack has already done
the semantic groundwork: the dark-tower excursion
(\texttt{futon5a/holes/excursions/E-the-dark-tower-2.md},
2026-06-02) maps $\otimes$ to independent parallel agent work,
$\parr$ to tightly-coupled co-work, and $\seq$ to the operator
$\to$ agent turn. \textbf{Warrant:} \warrant{registered} ---
candidate mechanism, not applied. \textbf{What demands it:} the War
Machine proof's load-bearing structural claims are
signalling-shaped and sit outside D1's vocabulary:

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The consent gate as a type.} ``Automated production,
operator-gated emission'' becomes a $\seq$-typing judgment: the
emission loop is well-typed only with the operator on the
signalling side of the outward edge. A structure in which the
machine signals outward without the operator is \emph{underivable},
not merely forbidden.
\item \emph{I4 exogeneity as no-signalling.} ``The fast loop cannot
force the glacial'' is a non-signalling constraint --- the dual
face of the same typing, stated in the logic rather than in the
guardrail code's comments.
\item \emph{Not-yet-answered as an open continuation.} A send whose
grade has not arrived is $A \seq X$ with the dual not yet supplied
--- the precise sense in which silence neither blocks nor flatters
the proof.
\item \emph{Conversion as a connective change.} The cold-chain
ladder's conversion rung --- a cold contact becoming a working
channel --- is composition upgrading from $\otimes$ toward $\parr$:
acquiring a channel, said in the grammar.
\item \emph{Standing as a linear resource.} The honest-standing
drop rule is a linear constraint --- do not use a hypothesis you do
not hold --- native to the multiplicative substrate D2 sits on.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{First-application contract} (the upgrade path from
\warrant{registered} to \warrant{mechanical}): type the War Machine
note's Figure~1 emission loop as a BV formula and check that the
guardrail invariants correspond to derivability facts --- the
operator-bypassing structure underivable; the I4 direction
non-signalling. BV provability is decidable (known NP-complete),
proof-search implementations exist in the deep-inference
literature, and instances at this scale are also hand-checkable ---
so the contract discharges mechanically, an afternoon-sized
exercise with a crisp pass/fail. \textbf{Hazards, named now:}
pomset logic and BV are provably distinct (Nguy\~{\^e}n \&
Stra{\ss}burger), and War Machine traces harvested as partial
orders from event data may be pomset-shaped --- \emph{which
self-dual seq the traces live in} is a question to flag, not
assume; and deep inference's context-rewriting reading of warrant
upgrades (``never re-argued, only re-certified'' as rewriting at
depth) is an attractive correspondence that remains
\warrant{registered} until something checkable hangs on it.

\subsection*{D3 --- Fong-inspired (registered, frontier)}

Synthetic causality in Markov categories: causal theories in the
Fong lineage, do-calculus stated without reference to a particular
graph semantics, and intervention as an endofunctor --- string-diagram
surgery (Jacobs--Kissinger--Zanasi), which the deep-research census
adopts as a design constraint: $do$ should be a functor on the
engine's own representation, not an ad hoc graph mutation.
\textbf{Warrant:} \warrant{registered} at best, and the census's
honesty clause governs: \emph{no categorical-causal-inference
result in the census has a runnable implementation} --- everything
at this tier is first-of-kind, validatable only by agreement with
D1 oracles on the shared fragment, with the stated blind spot (a
categorical bug that projects correctly to DAG-level answers).
\textbf{What would demand it:} claims that need probability and
higher-order structure \emph{together} --- quantitative versions of
the D2 claims (reply rates and review-round dynamics \emph{under}
signalling constraints), the value of memory amortized across
chains read as a second-order process (a comb), and any eventual
unification of the programme's two axes. The declared bridge
between the D2 axis and the D3 axis is the combs-in-Markov-categories
paper (arXiv:2404.02017), and the census's discipline holds here:
flagged deliberately, not on the short path.

The table's shape repeats the series' lesson at the meta-level: the
first two client proofs sit entirely in the implemented tier ---
the demand frontier was invisible until the third client arrived

each version can prove'' buys: the refusals locate the next version
before anyone argues about taste.

\section{Demand-paged logic acquisition}

The router's refusal log is a hunger log. A claim refused as
not-expressible-at-tier is a demand signal for the next rung,
exactly as a failed retrieval query is a demand signal for the next
memory and a hungry corpus query is a mining target: the same
pattern at a third altitude. Per WR-27 the loop is born
instrumented for its gain --- the fraction of typed refusals that
shape the next prover version is logged from day one, and a refusal
log that never changes the ladder is the uninstrumented-loop smell.
The corollary discipline: rungs are opened \emph{by demand}, never
by taste. D2 is being opened because the War Machine proof's
central claims are refused without it; D3 waits until a client
proof's refusal --- not an aesthetic preference for category theory
--- demands it.

\section{Honest boundaries}

D1's completeness claim is scoped: complete \emph{for the claims
the first two client proofs happen to make}, at DAG level,
oracle-triangulated --- not complete for causal inference at large.
D2 has semantic groundwork (the dark-tower mapping) and zero
applied instances; its first-application contract is written
precisely so that its first warrant upgrade is mechanical rather
than narrative. D3 has no runnable implementation anywhere in the
censused literature; anything built there is first-of-kind, with
the validation blind spot stated. The pomset-vs-BV question is
open and load-bearing for D2's trace semantics. And the
reflexivity guardrail bears repeating: every claim in this note
about a prover version is warranted by client-proof certificates
and executed checks, never by the version under discussion.

\section{What this is for}

The APM note argued the transferable object is the form; the War
Machine note showed what the form does when the grader is a human
institution. This note shows what it does when pointed at the
instrument: the prover stops being one thing that is good or bad
and becomes a ladder whose every rung carries the claims it can
certify and the refusals that demanded the rung above. ``The simple
Pearl-inspired one is enough for the APM model'' is now a row with
receipts; ``we need a Kissinger-inspired one for the War Machine''
is now five typed refusals and an afternoon-sized discharge
contract; ``maybe a Fong-inspired one for further capabilities''
is now a registered frontier with its bridge object named and its
validation blind spot admitted. When a client proof someday makes
a claim all three tiers refuse, the ladder will say so in its own
vocabulary --- which is the only way a research programme's
instruments stay the right size for its questions.
