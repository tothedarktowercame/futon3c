[lemma | m0002]

At each \(x\in X\), prove two separate obligations:

1. For every \(\rho\)-ball \(B_\rho(x,\varepsilon)\), find a basic product neighbourhood \(U\ni x\) with \(U\subseteq B_\rho(x,\varepsilon)\). The nontrivial point is the uncontrolled infinite tail; use \(|x_i-y_i|\le 1\) and choose finitely many coordinates so \(\sum_{i>N}2^{-i}<\varepsilon\), then control the finite head.

2. For every basic product neighbourhood \(U=\prod U_i\ni x\), with only finitely many \(U_i\ne[0,1]\), find \(\varepsilon>0\) such that \(B_\rho(x,\varepsilon)\subseteq U\). The nontrivial point is converting finitely many coordinatewise margins into one metric radius; for each constrained \(i\), choose \(\delta_i>0\) with the relative interval around \(x_i\) contained in \(U_i\), then take \(\varepsilon\le\min_i 2^{-i}\delta_i\).
