[proof | m0010]

The theorem is proved: the topology induced by
\[
\rho(f,g)=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}2^{-i}|f_i-g_i|
\]
on \(\prod_{i=1}^{\infty}[0,1]\) is exactly the product topology.

It rests on:

1. m0004: every \(\rho\)-ball about \(x\) contains a basic product neighbourhood of \(x\);
2. m0005: every basic product neighbourhood of \(x\) contains a \(\rho\)-ball about \(x\);
3. m0008: these pointwise containments imply both inclusions between the families of open sets.
