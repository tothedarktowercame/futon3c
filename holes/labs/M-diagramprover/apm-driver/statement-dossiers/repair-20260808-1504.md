### Declared repairs
The former encoding used `ℝ × ℝ`, whose Mathlib product norm is the sup norm.

two-dimensional Newtonian potential.

The printed Laplacian contains the typographical fragment `∂²_{x₁} += ∂²_{x₂}`;
this is read as the sum of the two second partial derivatives.

## m94J05 — gate: pending-review

### Source TeX
```tex
Let $f$ be a continuous real-valued function on the interval
$[1,2]$. Show thatif the maximum of $|f(t)|$ is sufficiently small,
then there is a unique real-valued function $x$ on $[1,2]$ that is
twice continuously differentiable and satisfies
$t^2x''(t)+2tx'(t)=f(t)+x(t)^2$ ($t\in [1,2]$) together with the

        ContinuousOn f (Icc 1 2) →

        ∃ x : ℝ → ℝ,
          IsM94J05Solution f x ∧
          (∀ t ∈ Icc (1 : ℝ) 2, |x t| ≤ r) ∧
          ∀ z : ℝ → ℝ,
            IsM94J05Solution f z →
            (∀ t ∈ Icc (1 : ℝ) 2, |z t| ≤ r) →
            EqOn z x (Icc 1 2) := by
```

### Declared repairs
The source's unrestricted uniqueness claim is false: even at zero forcing the
associated superlinear two-point Dirichlet problem has a nonzero large branch.
The conclusion is therefore stated as existence and uniqueness in a uniform
sup-norm ball, which is exactly the small branch controlled by the hinted
contraction argument.

Solutions are represented by ambient functions `ℝ → ℝ` to use Mathlib's
derivatives.  Consequently uniqueness is stated with `Set.EqOn` on `[1,2]`;
values outside the source interval are intentionally irrelevant.

## t01J04 — gate: pending-review

### Source TeX
```tex
Let $f:X\rightarrow Y$ be a smooth map between manifolds without
boundary. Given a point $y\in Y$ and a neighborhood $U\ni y$ in $Y$,
show that there is a neighborhood $V\ni y$ in $Y$ with closure
$\text{cl}(V)\subset U $ such that $f^{-1}(\text{cl}(V))$ is a
manifold with boundary.

\soln{Every point has an arbitrarily small closed neighborhood whose
inverse image under a diffeomorphism is a manifold with boundary.}
```

### Declared repairs
Mathlib's manifold typeclasses do not include the usual Hausdorff separation
convention.  `T2Space` is therefore required for both manifolds, and
`RegularSpace Y` is required explicitly for the neighborhood-shrinking step
`closure V ⊆ U`.  Without it, the line with doubled origin gives a
counterexample even for the identity map.
