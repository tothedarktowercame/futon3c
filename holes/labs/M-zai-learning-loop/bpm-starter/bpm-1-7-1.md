# BPM 1.7.1 (Sp80)
Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be the unique function such that $f(x) = x$ if $-\pi \le x < \pi$ and $f(x + 2n\pi) = f(x)$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
1. Prove that the Fourier series of $f$ is $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n+1} 2 \sin nx}{n}$.
2. Prove that the series does not converge uniformly.
3. For each $x \in \mathbb{R}$, find the sum of the series.
