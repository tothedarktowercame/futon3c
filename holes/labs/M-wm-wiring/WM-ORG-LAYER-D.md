## What a generated third strip needs from the script

- **Its input is `wm-org-layer.edn` itself.** `:roots`, and `:calls` with
  `:order`, `:conditional` and `:constructs`, give a rooted, ordered tree; a
  strip drawn from it replaces the hand lane table.
- **One depth per box.** A box called from several places (`r6-cascade-lane`
  from construction, the tick and the ask hand-off; `c8-entry` from four
  places) needs a rule. Two candidates: its FIRST caller in flight order (the
  order of `flight-run`'s calls), or all callers drawn as edges to one box.
  The script should emit one; I would take first-in-flight-order, so each box
  sits once and the other calls become edges.
- **Hand-offs kept as their own kind of edge.** Where a caller builds a
  closure (`:constructs`) and another invokes it (`:via [:handoff k]`), the
  strip should draw the invocation as the call and the construction as a thin
  "builds" mark. Otherwise the driver looks as if it runs every step.
- **Conditionals as edge labels.** A predicate such as `(when read-fn)`,
  `(if (empty? problems)) else` or `(defmethod source-wants :a-exits)` is
  short enough to print on its edge.
- **Hand-checked edges drawn distinctly.** HTTP, subprocess and bell calls
  (`:hand true`) are not read from text, and if the other side moves they go
  stale without the script noticing. The generator could check the evidence
  sites still exist, e.g. the route string at `http.clj:9295`.
- **The unplaced list drawn as islands.** Especially the target-field boxes,
  which have code but no production caller: that is the open outer loop.

## Limits

- **Resolution is textual.** A var passed as data under a key not ending in
  `-fn`, a call through `apply`, `resolve` or a multimethod dispatch value the
  script cannot see, or a dynamic var bound elsewhere are all missed.
  `defmethod` bodies are followed, and each is labelled with its dispatch value.
- **A closure-returning helper that is not handed off under a `-fn` key is
  credited to the site that calls the helper**, not to the site that invokes
  its closure.
- **`:order` ranks a callee by its first appearance in the caller's body**,
  including through helpers. For calls in different branches, it is text
  order, not execution order.
- **Nothing was run, and the map was not edited.** The futon3c files (for
  `click-start` and `clock-in`) were read at bc45a2a0.
