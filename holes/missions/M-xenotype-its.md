## The four-layer stack (Joe's mapping)

| Layer | Inhabitant | Product | Witness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phenotype** | zai runners | proof behavior on problems | compiler (`lake`) |
| **Genotype** | codex sorry/literature layer | the formal corpus (lemmas, constructions) | compiler + axiom check |
| **Exotype** | the Scribe (memory mining) | the typed memory corpus / niche | receipts + review |
| **Xenotype** | the human (Joe) | evaluative frame: **"can you explain this to me?"** | the human — deliberately non-mechanizable |

The xenotype adds the QC dimension the stack is otherwise blind to:
the compiler checks *derivability*; explanation checks *structure*.
Axiom-clean-but-inexplicable (tactic golf) is a real state, and a loop
optimizing only the mechanical witness drifts there — the dark-room
problem at corpus level. Per the Baldwin-ratchet discipline: internalise
the pressure, require demonstrated adapted behavior; evidence = changed
phenotype (future proofs that explain better AND prove better).

## Drill-down without cheating (the ITS inversion)

Classic ITS drill-down risks leaking answers. Here the answer is
machine-verified and public; the scarce thing is understanding. Every
drill-down "no" lands in exactly one of four typed bins:

1. **:explanation-edge** — the step decomposes; a new typed link
   (proof-step → lemma → concept) enters the graph. Connectivity food
   of a NEW edge type.
2. **:ancillary-target** — the explanation needs a lemma the proof
   golfed past → genotype work (a queue row, demand 1, ancillary to
   the prelim corpus per se).
3. **:capability-gap** — the explainer lacks the move → exotype work
   (a memory/pattern to mint).
4. **:xenotype-datum** — where the human's understanding sticks or
   slides → profile signal (below).

Joe's prediction — the answer to "can you explain this" is often
"no" — is what makes this an engine: every "no" is typed demand.

## The 157-scaffold policy (what makes this "policy")

**Demand-driven resurrection, ported up a layer.** A scaffold sorry is
sequenced when an explanation drill-down *touches* it; it stays dormant
otherwise; a scaffold region no drill-down reaches across the campaign
has empirically demonstrated its archivability. No bulk triage. Exact
mirror of the geno-layer's frontier demand-promotion: frontiers
accumulate demand from blocked proofs; scaffold items accumulate demand
from blocked *explanations*. The ITS sessions generate the sequencing
signal as exhaust, the way receipts generate Ψ.

## Xenotype profile mining

Explanation sessions are sessions: harvested, scribed, receipted.
Stratify outcomes by terrain facet → the "Joe is better at topology
than algebra" profile is a per-facet meter; "figure out why" = the
scribe mining drill-down transcripts (which decompositions get asked
for, where explanations stick). Higher-level strategy patterns are the
xeno layer's memory lane, useful down-stack (packet framing,
curriculum selection).

**Guard (binding, gamification discipline)**: profile meters MEASURE,
never TARGET. They inform curriculum and framing; they are never a
score, for the human least of all.
