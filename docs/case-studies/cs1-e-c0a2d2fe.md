## Five gaps, one memory

1. **Recorded ≠ attributed.** The store said its author was
   "problem-peripheral" — the plumbing, not the seat. The reviewer≠
   depositor gate was reading a fiction. (W.55/W.61 check C1; fixed by
   P21, `af9e0cb0`: author = seat, via = plumbing.)

2. **Recorded ≠ reachable (by design collision).** Under the operator's
   attach-then-review ruling, recall surfaces only reviewed memories;
   promotion sat AFTER student-attempts in the phase chain. A mid-cycle
   deposit was invisible to its own cycle's student BY CONSTRUCTION —
   two correct rules composing into a wall. (W.44; fixed by P13's
   :promote-solver phase, `de8fd3b8`, + P16's witnessed eligibility
   union, `0e86901c`; check C2/C3.)

3. **Reachable ≠ reached (the channel surprise).** Push recall never
   surfaced it — but the f7 student FOUND IT ANYWAY via pull
   (memory_search + memory_read), adopted the route, and converted it
   into five committed part-1 theorems before its runner died mid-report.
   The founding thesis — solver knowledge through the store to a student
   who uses it — happened here first, invisibly. (W.55, guide's
   transcript salvage; the student branch persists in apm-lean.)

4. **Used ≠ measured.** Pull-only dispatches generated no offer
   entities, so the working channel produced zero receipts: the cycle
   record said "no memory use" about the frame in which memory use
   worked. (W.55; fixed by P17 pull receipts, `eb7a3e03`; check C4.)

5. **Measured ≠ safely runnable.** The runner's interior caps (not the
   believed 30-minute wall clock, which no longer existed) killed the
   using-student mid-report — work done, unreported. (W.55/W.59; fixed
   by P19: 60-minute wall clock with a five-minute reporting reserve,
   `77f88406`.)

## What the case study argues (for the whitepaper)

- The five predicates are separable and must be separately witnessed;
  any memory design that conflates them will report its own health
  wrongly in whichever direction flatters it.
- The pull channel — the one that worked — was the UNMEASURED one. Usage
  instrumentation must cover the channels agents actually prefer, not
  the channels the design intended them to prefer.
- Every gap was found by inhabitation (a fresh agent doing its carded
  job and reporting honestly), not by inspection; and every fix was a
  small reviewed packet landed within hours. The operator-corrections-
  as-corpus method (§7a) generalizes: the memory system's own users are
  its best auditors, when the protocol makes their findings cheap to
  report and expensive to ignore.
- e-c0a2d2fe itself remains unpromoted (a98A01-specific route; the
  generalizable technique went to the shelf as
  math-formalization/radialize-via-gauge-rescale et al.) — a deliberate
  curation choice (W.37-era) that the cascade layer (plan §9) will
  revisit: as a SPECIALIZATION of shelved patterns, its natural place is
  a leaf under upstream parents, reachable by descent rather than by
  lexical luck.
